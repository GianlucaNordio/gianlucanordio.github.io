Built in one weekend at the {Tech: Europe} hackathon in Paris with Lorenzo Cazzador, Alberto Basaglia and Manuel Lovo. HireMind is an AI agent that conducts behavioural interviews: it generates questions for a specific job and holds the conversation through an adaptive avatar. The team won the MakiPeople track and placed second overall, among more than 30 teams.
