Team Hugo's entry for DTU RoboCup 2025, built with Luca Boggiani, Simone Carletti, Tomás Venda, Daniil Losev and Andrei Fărcaș. The work covered sensor fusion, PID tuning, real-time navigation and obstacle avoidance, and making all of it reliable under unpredictable conditions. We qualified second among 21 teams. A last-minute change between the two finals runs introduced a bug that cost us a podium place, a lesson in how fragile complex systems are under pressure.
