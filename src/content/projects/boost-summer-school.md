A highly selective two-week intensive summer school in Bologna, organized by Fondazione Elicsir and supported by Schmidt Sciences, with lecturers from Google, Cornell, UT Austin, the University of Chicago and several Italian universities. The conversations with professors outside the lectures, on AI and cryptography among other topics, were as valuable as the lectures. Working with peers from very different backgrounds showed how much a diverse team adds.
