A four-day hackathon organized by NNE and ClearEdge3D, with Lorenzo Cazzador and Alberto Basaglia. We built a model to classify and segment 3D point clouds, working directly on the clouds with Open3D. The hard part was the real-world data: a strong class imbalance and very few labelled examples.
