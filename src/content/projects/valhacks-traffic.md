A hackathon at DTU Skylab, organized by BEST Copenhagen with Accenture as main sponsor, with Alberto Basaglia, Lorenzo Cazzador, Paolo Nalato and Manuel Lovo. The challenge was to reduce congestion in the San Francisco Bay Area from geospatial data. Comparing traffic models with the real data, we found the city close to a non-linear drop in congestion: a small cut in cars at specific rush hours could greatly improve traffic flow. We proposed targeted remote work and flexible hours for companies in the affected areas.
