title: Rosbag analyzer
year: 2026
description: A tool for diagnosing ROS 2 bags, where every finding is backed by evidence from the data. Work in progress.
tech: [ROS 2, Python, LLMs]
---

An LLM reading a bag directly may invent problems that aren't there. This tool makes every finding point back to evidence in the recorded data, so a diagnosis can be checked rather than trusted. Work in progress.
