A group project for the Robotics course at DTU. A camera picks out a drawn line in a picture, and the arm follows it. The pipeline covers camera calibration with a chessboard, forward and inverse kinematics, and trajectory generation. The [article](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1clXluzws6pSjspV3OfZWMzWNSk1mfRmA/view?usp=sharing) describes the method, and the link below is a video of the real arm.
