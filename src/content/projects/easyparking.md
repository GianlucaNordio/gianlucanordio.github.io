A group project of three for the Computer Vision course at the University of Padua. From a sequence of images of a parking lot, the system detects the parking spots, segments the cars, classifies each spot as free or occupied and draws a minimap of the lot. Detection and segmentation are scored with mean average precision and intersection over union.
